About the space

This lounge is perfect for any social gathering, celebration or meeting (retreat). Flexible seating of couches, club chairs and tables works for casual mingling or sit-down dining, and the pool table will keep the crowd entertained.

The space is semi-private and warmly lit with decorative accent lighting. It has a wet bar, a fireplace, a balcony and plenty of countertop space for catering. It's inside a residential community, so please keep noise at a considerate level.
